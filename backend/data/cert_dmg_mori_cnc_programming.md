# Basics of Programming & Operating CNC Turning & Milling Machines

## Certificate of Excellence Details
- **Recipient**: Yousef alsayed abdelfatah abo elkhear
- **University Affiliation**: Misr University for Science and Technology (MUST)
- **Training Course**: Basics of Programming & Operating CNC Turning & Milling Machines
- **Certifying Body**: AOI Academy (Arab Organization for Industrialization) certified by **DMG MORI**
- **Duration**: September 11, 2022 to September 22, 2022

## Technical Coursework & Hands-On Machine Time
- **G-Code & M-Code Programming**: Manual part programming for linear interpolation (`G01`), circular interpolation (`G02`/`G03`), canned drilling cycles, and threading.
- **CNC Lathe (Turning) Operations**: Outer diameter turning, facing, grooving, parting, and threading operations.
- **CNC Milling Operations**: 3-axis surface milling, pocketing, contouring, and hole drilling.
- **Tool Setup & Machine Calibration**: Work Coordinate Systems (`G54`–`G59`), tool length offset measurement, touch-off probing, and spindle speed/feed rate calculations.
- **Manufacturing Precision**: Adhering to micrometric engineering tolerances and industrial safety standards on heavy industrial machining centers.
