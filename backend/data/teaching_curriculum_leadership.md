# Computer Science Teaching, Pedagogy & Mentorship

Since May 2026, Yousef Alsayed Abdelfattah has served as an English-speaking Programming & AI Instructor at **iSchool**, educating youth in Cairo, Egypt.

## 1. Pedagogical Scope & Impact
- Instructed **over 30 students** across twelve grade levels (Grades 1 through 12).
- Taught entirely in **English**, conveying intricate algorithmic and programming concepts clearly to diverse age brackets.
- Curricula spans:
  - **Early Elementary (Grades 1–2)**: Computational thinking, visual technology concepts, Minecraft educational coding, block-based logic.
  - **Upper Elementary (Grades 3–4)**: 2D game development, block-to-script transitions, game logic with AI, chatbot construction ("Create Your Own Siri").
  - **Middle School (Grades 5–6)**: Python programming fundamentals, chatbot logic with data encryption and hashing, UI/UX design thinking and wireframing.
  - **Junior High (Grades 7–8)**: Mobile application development (App Inventor/React Native), full web development with semantic HTML5, CSS3 styling, and interactive JavaScript.
  - **High School (Grades 9–12)**: 2D/3D game design using game engines, data science pipelines with Python, NumPy, Pandas, data visualization, and applied machine learning.

## 2. Professional Instructor Certifications (18 Modules)
Yousef successfully completed the rigorous iSchool instructor qualification curriculum certified by:
- **STEM.org Accredited Educational Experience**
- **IAIDL (International AI Driving License)**
- **Education Alliance Finland (Certified Pedagogical Quality)**
- **kidSAFE Seal Program**

Each module requires mastery of pedagogical delivery, differentiated instruction, student engagement strategies, project-based assessment, and subject-matter technical expertise.

## 3. Professional Mentorship: Deloitte NextStep Program
- Participated in the **Deloitte NextStep Mentorship Program** (July 2026 Cohort, Deloitte Innovation Hub).
- Engaged with senior enterprise technology consultants on industry delivery methodologies, team leadership, client communication, and agile solution consulting.
