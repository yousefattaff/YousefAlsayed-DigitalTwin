# Practical Training in Car Assembly & Maintenance: Arab American Vehicles (AAV)

## Certificate of Excellence Details
- **Recipient**: Yousef Alsayed Abdelfatah Abo El Khear
- **University Affiliation**: Misr University for Science & Technology (MUST)
- **Training Program**: Practical Training in "Car Assembly & Maintenance"
- **Host Company**: Arab American Vehicles Company (AAV), Arab Institute for Advanced Technology (AIAT), Arab Organization for Industrialization (AOI)
- **Quality Certification**: TÜV AUSTRIA EN ISO 9001:2015 (Certificate No.: 0718100067035)
- **Dates**: September 19, 2021 to September 30, 2021
- **Signed by**: Eng. Ahmed Refaat, Director of AIAT

## Engineering Exposure & Practical Experience
- **Vehicle Assembly Line Operations**: Observing and practicing vehicle chassis assembly, body-in-white mating, engine mounting, and interior fitting.
- **Mechanical Systems Diagnostics**: Troubleshooting suspension systems, drivetrain assemblies, braking hydraulics, and cooling circuits.
- **Quality Assurance & Inspection**: Industrial testing protocols in accordance with ISO 9001:2015 standards, torque verification on safety-critical fasteners, and final vehicle testing cycles.
- **Teamwork in Manufacturing**: Collaborative execution within high-standard factory floors alongside production engineers and assembly line technicians.
