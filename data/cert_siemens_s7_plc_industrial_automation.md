# TIA Portal Programming with Siemens S7-1200 & S7-1500

## Certificate of Excellence Details
- **Recipient**: Yousef Alsayed Abdelfatah
- **University Affiliation**: Misr University for Science and Technology (MUST)
- **Training Course**: TIA – Programming1 with S7 "1200 & 1500"
- **Certifying Body**: AOI Academy certified by **SIEMENS** (Arab Institute for Advanced Technology)
- **Duration**: September 10, 2023 to September 21, 2023
- **Signed by**: Mr. Maged Mohamed Mahmoud, Director of the Arab Institute for Advanced Technology

## Industrial Automation Curriculum
- **Totally Integrated Automation (TIA) Portal**: Complete environment setup, hardware configuration, and network topology.
- **PLC Hardware Configurations**: Siemens S7-1200 and high-performance S7-1500 CPU modules, digital/analog I/O cards, and PROFINET communication.
- **Ladder Logic (LAD) & Function Block Diagrams (FBD)**: Designing robust sequential control logic, latching, timers (TON, TOF, TP), up/down counters, and comparator blocks.
- **Organizational Blocks (OBs), Function Blocks (FBs) & Data Blocks (DBs)**: Modular, structured industrial software design with parameterized inputs, outputs, and static data block storage.
- **Industrial Debugging & Diagnostics**: Online monitoring, forcing I/O tags, troubleshooting hardware communication faults, and automated emergency safety loops.
