# Robotics, Embedded Systems & Industrial Automation

With a degree in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Automation Engineering from Misr University for Science and Technology (MUST), Yousef's foundation bridges low-level hardware registers, industrial factory automation, and autonomous robotic navigation.

## 1. Autonomous Mobile Robotics (ROS1 & ROS2)
- **R2-WAITER Autonomous Service Robot**:
  - Flagship project awarded 3rd place at the International Gathering of Artificial Intelligence in Egypt.
  - ROS2 navigation stack (`Nav2`), SLAM mapping, sensor fusion between 2D LIDAR and Intel RealSense D435i depth cameras.
  - Deployed on NVIDIA Jetson edge compute with local hardware acceleration.
  - Multi-threaded customer ordering interface with dynamic animated facial OLED emotion expressions.
- **Simulation & Gazebo**:
  - Gazebo world simulation, URDF / Xacro robot kinematics modeling, TurtleBot3 prototyping.

## 2. Embedded Systems & Microcontroller Engineering
- **IMT Embedded Systems Diploma (March 2023)**:
  - Advanced embedded C programming following MISRA standards.
  - Register-level peripheral driver development for Microchip ATmega32: DIO, External Interrupts (EXTI), Timers/PWM, ADC, USART, SPI, and I2C protocols.
  - Interfacing with LCD displays, keypad matrices, EEPROM memories, and sensor arrays.

## 3. Industrial Factory Automation & Precision Machining
- **Siemens S7-1200 & S7-1500 PLC Programming (September 2023)**:
  - Certified by AOI Academy & SIEMENS in Cairo.
  - TIA Portal programming, structured ladder logic (LAD), Function Block Diagrams (FBD), hardware configuration, diagnostics, and factory floor troubleshooting.
- **DMG MORI CNC Turning & Milling (September 2022)**:
  - Certified by AOI Academy & DMG MORI.
  - G-code and M-code programming, CNC lathe and vertical milling operation, coordinate system setup, tool offset calibration, precision tolerances, and industrial manufacturing workflows.
- **Arab American Vehicles (AAV / AOI) Automotive Training (September 2021)**:
  - Certified training in car assembly, vehicle mechanical systems, assembly line ergonomics, and TÜV AUSTRIA ISO 9001:2015 quality assurance.
