# What was your role at Outlier? (en)

I worked as an AI Software Engineer at Outlier from November 2024 to January 2025. I evaluated over 50 LLM responses daily across more than six domains and contributed reinforcement learning from human feedback, or RLHF.

# How did you investigate hallucinations and reasoning? (en)

At Outlier, I developed more than 20 prompt-engineering variations to identify hallucination and reasoning issues. This gave me practical experience examining how a model's answers change with the prompt and assessing the quality of those outputs.

# Did you review other people's evaluation work? (en)

I conducted quality reviews on over 50 analyst submissions at Outlier. I gave feedback to improve annotation consistency in the human-feedback workflow. That work involved evaluating responses and reviewing the quality of the evaluation itself.
