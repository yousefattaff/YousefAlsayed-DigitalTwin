# How did your engineering journey start? (en)

I started in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Automation Engineering at Misr University for Science and Technology, studying from September 2019 to June 2024. Much of my early work involved Embedded C, microcontrollers, and autonomous robots with ROS. Working with hardware taught me to build systems that hold up in practice.

# Why did you move from mechatronics into AI? (en)

I became increasingly interested in the intelligence side of robotics. That led me from embedded systems and autonomous robots into AI engineering and workflow automation. My recent learning and projects focus on agents, document retrieval, smaller local models, and the backend services needed to turn them into useful applications.

# What connects your different roles? (en)

At Arche Robotics I build an AI agent for live robot-log monitoring. At iSchool I teach programming and AI in English. Earlier, I evaluated LLM responses and contributed RLHF feedback at Outlier. These roles give me hands-on experience building AI systems, explaining technical ideas, and evaluating model outputs.
