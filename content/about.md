# About Yousef Alsayed Abdelfattah

I am Yousef Alsayed Abdelfattah, an AI engineer based in Cairo, Egypt. I build practical AI solutions, LLM applications, multi-agent workflows, and automations. I use AI-assisted development and coding agents alongside Python engineering, testing, and evaluation to turn ideas into working systems.

I started in embedded C, microcontrollers, and autonomous robots with ROS while studying Mechatronics, Robotics, and Automation Engineering at Misr University for Science and Technology (MUST), from September 2019 to June 2024. That engineering foundation led me into AI development and workflow automation.

I am currently an Artificial Intelligence Intern at Arche Robotics, where I work on a real-time AI monitoring agent for live robot logs and anomaly detection. Alongside that, I teach Python, AI, JavaScript, and UI/UX fundamentals in English at iSchool. Previously, I evaluated LLM outputs and contributed RLHF feedback at Outlier.
