# Where did you study and what was your GPA or CGPA? (en)

I earned a Bachelor of Science in Mechatronics, Robotics, and Automation Engineering at Misr University for Science and Technology, or MUST. I studied from September 2019 to June 2024. My public resume lists a CGPA of 3.34.

# What did you learn in embedded systems? (en)

My early engineering work included Embedded C, microcontrollers, and autonomous robots with ROS. I completed an IMT embedded systems diploma in March 2023. My ATmega32 drivers and applications project applies that background through small C programs and device-driver exercises.

# What industrial training did you complete? (en)

I trained in car assembly and maintenance at AOI Training Academy in September 2021, CNC turning and milling at DMG MORI in September 2022, and Siemens PLC automation in September 2023. The Siemens training covered TIA Portal with S7-1200 and S7-1500 PLCs.
