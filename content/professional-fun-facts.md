# Tell me a fun fact about your engineering background. (en)

My engineering journey goes from Embedded C and microcontrollers to a restaurant service robot and AI agents. For my graduation project, I worked in a six-person team and built the operator interface that sent navigation goals to R2-WAITER. The project earned third place at an AI gathering in Egypt.

# Is there an interesting connection between your projects? (en)

Two of my projects connect engineering with dining: R2-WAITER is a restaurant service robot, and EgyptianSavoryScout helps people find Egyptian restaurants. The restaurant finder covers 1,000+ dining spots and uses TF-IDF retrieval with a locally fine-tuned Qwen2 model to explain its recommendations.

# What is something people might not expect about your work? (en)

Alongside building an AI robot-monitoring agent at Arche Robotics, I teach programming in English at iSchool. My documented teaching experience spans over 30 students across Grades 1–12. I have also trained in car assembly, CNC machining, and industrial PLC automation.
